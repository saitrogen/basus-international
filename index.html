About

A Sourcing Partner Built for Battery, Electrification & Power

BASUS International is an India–Europe sourcing and supply-chain company focused on the battery, electrification and power distribution sector.

We work with European procurement and engineering teams to source custom-specified components from Indian manufacturers — from initial technical requirement through to production coordination and export.

We are building a focused network of qualified Indian manufacturing partners and assess each sourcing requirement individually. What we offer is domain focus, a structured process, and straightforward communication at every stage.

Focus Areas

What We Source

We concentrate on a specific segment of the battery, electrification and power supply chain. Custom-specified, technically demanding components for commercial EVs, battery systems, BESS and industrial power applications.

01

Custom Copper & Aluminium Busbars

Rigid and flexible busbar assemblies manufactured to customer drawing. Copper (ETP, OFC) or aluminium. Surface treatments — tin, silver and nickel plating — available to specification. Applications include switchgear, power electronics and EV battery systems.

Busbars
02

HV/LV Cable Assemblies

High-voltage and low-voltage cable assemblies for industrial and vehicular applications. Custom lengths, connector types, shielding and insulation specified by the customer. We source to your drawing, not from a standard catalogue.

How We Work

A Structured Sourcing Process

We follow a defined, step-by-step approach for every enquiry. Each stage has a clear scope and output — so you know where you are and what comes next throughout the process.

01

Customer Requirement

You share drawings, specifications or technical requirements. We review scope, quantities and target timeline together before proceeding.

02

Supplier Identification

We identify Indian manufacturers with relevant capability and capacity for your component type and volume requirement.

03

Capability Evaluation

Shortlisted suppliers are assessed against your technical and quality requirements before we proceed to commercial quotation.

04

Quotation

A clear commercial proposal covering unit price, tooling where applicable, lead time and shipping terms.

05

Sample / Prototype

First-article samples or prototypes are arranged for your review and approval before any production commitment is made.

06

Production & Delivery

We coordinate production scheduling, pre-shipment communication and export documentation through to delivery.
