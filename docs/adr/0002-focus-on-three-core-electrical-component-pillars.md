# 0002. Focus on Three Core Electrical Component Pillars

BASUS International streamlines its product portfolio to three core pillars: Custom Copper Busbars, HV/LV Cable Assemblies, and Custom Electrical Interconnects & Flexible Connectors. Complex battery management system (BMS) wiring harnesses and generic electrification items are removed from core positioning to match high-confidence Indian manufacturing partner capabilities and avoid dilution of industrial focus.
