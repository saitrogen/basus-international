About Us

A Sourcing Partner Built for Electrification

BASUS International is an India–Europe sourcing and supply-chain company, currently developing qualified manufacturing partnerships in the electrification and power distribution sector.

We work with European buyers — procurement managers, engineering teams and product developers — to source custom-specified components from Indian manufacturers, handling the process from technical requirement through to delivery coordination.

We are at an early stage of building our supplier network, and we are honest about that. What we bring is focused domain knowledge, a structured sourcing approach, and a genuine commitment to matching European specification standards with Indian manufacturing capability.

Our Focus Areas

What We Source

We concentrate on a specific, technically demanding segment of the electrification supply chain — custom power distribution and battery interconnect components for industrial, commercial vehicle and energy storage applications.

01
🔩

Custom Copper & Aluminium Busbars

Rigid and flexible busbar assemblies manufactured to customer drawing. Materials: copper (ETP, OFC) or aluminium. Surface treatments available: tin, silver and nickel plating. Applications include switchgear, power electronics and EV battery systems.

02
⚡

HV/LV Cable Assemblies

High-voltage and low-voltage cable assemblies for industrial and vehicular applications. Custom lengths, connector types, shielding and insulation to customer specification. Sourced to your drawing, not from a stock catalogue.

03
🔋

Battery & BMS Wiring Harnesses

Wiring harnesses for battery packs and Battery Management Systems. Designed to customer schematic with appropriate wire gauge, connector selection and routing specification for your application.

04
🔌

Battery Electrical Interconnects

Cell-level and module-level interconnect components for battery pack assembly. Includes rigid busbars, flexible interconnects and inter-cell connectors, sourced to your dimensional and electrical specification.

05
⚙️

Selected Custom Electrification Components

Other custom-specified electrical and electrification components within our sourcing capability. Each enquiry is assessed on its own merits — reach out to discuss whether your requirement fits our scope.

How We Work

A Structured Sourcing Process

We follow a defined, step-by-step approach for every enquiry — from understanding your technical requirement through to coordinating production and delivery.

01

Customer Requirement

Share your drawings, specifications or technical requirements. We review scope, quantities and target timeline together.

02

Supplier Identification

We identify Indian manufacturers with relevant capability and capacity for your specific component type and volume.

03

Capability Evaluation

Shortlisted suppliers are assessed against your technical and quality requirements before proceeding to commercial quotation.

04

Quotation

We compile a clear commercial proposal covering unit price, tooling costs where applicable, lead time and shipping terms.

05

Sample / Prototype

First-article samples or prototypes are arranged for your review and approval before any production commitment is made.

06

Production & Delivery Coordination

We coordinate production scheduling, pre-shipment communication and export documentation through to delivery.
