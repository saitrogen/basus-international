About Us

A Sourcing Partner Built for Electrification

BASUS International is an India–Europe sourcing and supply-chain company, currently developing qualified manufacturing partnerships in the electrification and power distribution sector.

We work with European buyers — procurement managers, engineering teams and product developers — to source custom-specified components from Indian manufacturers, handling the process from technical requirement through to delivery coordination.

We are building our supplier network, and we are honest about that. What we bring is focused domain knowledge, a structured sourcing approach, and a genuine commitment to matching European specification standards with Indian manufacturing capability.

⚡

Focused Domain

Electrification components only — busbars, cable assemblies, wiring harnesses. No broad commodity catalogue. No scope creep.

🔗

Structured Process

Every enquiry follows a defined workflow from technical requirement to delivery. Clear steps, clear communication at each stage.

🤝

Our Focus Areas

What We Source

A specific, technically demanding segment of the electrification supply chain — custom power distribution and battery interconnect components for industrial, commercial vehicle and energy storage applications.

01
🔩

Custom Copper & Aluminium Busbars

Rigid and flexible busbar assemblies to customer drawing. Materials: copper (ETP, OFC) or aluminium. Surface treatments: tin, silver and nickel plating. Applications: switchgear, power electronics, EV battery systems.

02
⚡

HV/LV Cable Assemblies

High-voltage and low-voltage cable assemblies for industrial and vehicular applications. Custom lengths, connector types, shielding and insulation to your specification. Sourced to your drawing.

03
🔋

Battery & BMS Wiring Harnesses

Wiring harnesses for battery packs and Battery Management Systems. Designed to customer schematic with appropriate wire gauge, connector selection and routing specification.

04
🔌

Battery Electrical Interconnects

Cell-level and module-level interconnect components for battery pack assembly. Includes rigid busbars, flexible interconnects and inter-cell connectors to your dimensional specification.

⚙️

Selected Custom Electrification Components

Other custom-specified electrical components within our sourcing capability. Each enquiry assessed on its own merits — reach out to discuss whether your requirement fits our scope.

How We Work

A Structured Sourcing Process

A defined, step-by-step approach for every enquiry — from understanding your technical requirement through to coordinating production and delivery.

01

Customer Requirement

Share your drawings, specs or technical requirements. We review scope, quantities and target timeline together.

02

Supplier Identification

We identify Indian manufacturers with relevant capability and capacity for your specific component type and volume.

03

Capability Evaluation

Shortlisted suppliers are assessed against your technical and quality requirements before proceeding to quotation.

04

Quotation

A clear commercial proposal covering unit price, tooling where applicable, lead time and shipping terms.

05

Sample / Prototype

First-article samples arranged for your review and approval before any production commitment is made.

06

Production & Delivery

We coordinate production scheduling, pre-shipment communication and export documentation through to delivery.
