Corporate Overview

An International Commercial Supplier of Custom Electrical Components

BASUS International operates as a commercial supplier connecting qualified Indian manufacturing capabilities with industrial customers worldwide.

Rather than acting as a broker or commission-based intermediary, BASUS directly manages the commercial interface. We coordinate customer technical requirements, qualified manufacturing partner evaluation, commercial quotations, quality documentation, production follow-up, and international cross-border supply.

Component production is executed by selected, vetted Indian manufacturing partners, while BASUS delivers single-point commercial accountability to international buyers.

Vision

To build BASUS International into a trusted global commercial supply partner connecting qualified Indian manufacturing capabilities with industrial customers worldwide.

Mission

To simplify international industrial supply by coordinating customer requirements, qualified manufacturing selection, commercial quotations, quality documentation, production follow-up and cross-border delivery for custom electrical components.

Core Product Portfolio

Engineered Electrical Components

Our capabilities are focused on custom-specified, technically demanding electrical conductors and interconnect assemblies built to customer drawings, specifications, and international industrial standards.

01

Custom Copper Busbars

Rigid and flexible copper busbar assemblies manufactured to customer drawings and specifications. High-conductivity electrolytic copper (ETP, OFC) with custom CNC punching, bending, edge conditioning, surface plating (tin, silver, nickel), and dielectric insulation. Engineered for switchgear, power distribution units, inverters, and high-current electrical platforms. Aluminium busbar configurations available upon specification.

Busbars
02

HV/LV Cable Assemblies

High-voltage and low-voltage cable assemblies engineered for demanding vehicular and industrial electrical systems. Custom conductor routing, shielding, wire gauges, specialized temperature insulation, and heavy-duty industrial connectors fabricated strictly to customer schematics and environmental requirements.

Cable Assemblies
03

Custom Electrical Interconnects & Flexible Connectors

Engineered interconnect components, laminated copper connectors, braided flexible jumpers, and press-fit contact terminals. Designed to absorb thermal expansion, mechanical vibration, and dynamic stresses in battery systems, power conversion equipment, and OEM electrical enclosures.

Interconnects
Industries We Serve

Built for Mission-Critical Electrical Applications

We supply custom electrical components to original equipment manufacturers, Tier-1 suppliers, and system integrators across high-growth electrification and power sectors.

01

EV Charging

High-current DC busbars, liquid/air-cooled power cables, and flexible interconnects for commercial electric vehicle charging infrastructure and power distribution units.

02

Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)

Rack-level and container-level copper busbars, flexible inter-module jumpers, and HV power cabling engineered for utility-scale and commercial energy storage.

Custom electrical connections, busbar systems, and heavy-duty power assemblies for electrified industrial machinery, motor drives, and automation systems.

04

Power Equipment

05

Related Electrical OEM Applications

Drawing-driven custom conductors, stamped electrical terminals, and cable harnesses engineered to customer-specific OEM design criteria and compliance standards.
