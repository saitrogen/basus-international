# BASUS International Domain Model

BASUS International is an international commercial supplier of custom electrical components, connecting qualified Indian manufacturing capabilities with industrial customers worldwide.

## Language

**Commercial Supplier**:
An organization that contracts directly with buyers to coordinate technical requirements, manufacturing qualification, commercial terms, quality documentation, and international delivery.
_Avoid_: Sourcing agent, intermediary, broker, trading company, middleman, commission agent

**Manufacturing Partner**:
An independent, qualified Indian production facility vetted against project-specific technical and quality standards.
_Avoid_: Vendor, factory owned by BASUS, supplier database

**Supply Requirement**:
A customer's comprehensive project package comprising engineering drawings, material specifications, volume, compliance standards, and delivery terms.
_Avoid_: RFQ, buy enquiry, lead, purchase order

**Markets We Are Developing**:
Target geographical regions (Europe, United Kingdom, Middle East, North America, etc.) where BASUS is actively establishing commercial supply routes.
_Avoid_: Global branch offices, established international network, regional headquarters

**Partner Compliance & Certification**:
Quality management and industry certifications (e.g., ISO 9001, IATF 16949, ISO 14001, IEC) held directly by the vetted manufacturing partner facility.
_Avoid_: Our certifications, BASUS-certified facilities, company certifications
