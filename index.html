Corporate Overview

One accountable interface.
Independent manufacturing.

BASUS International operates as a commercial supplier connecting qualified Indian manufacturing capabilities with industrial customers worldwide.

BASUS directly manages the commercial interface. We coordinate technical requirements, manufacturing partner evaluation, quotations, quality documentation, production follow-up and cross-border supply.

Production is performed by independent Indian manufacturing partners selected against project requirements. BASUS is your commercial point of contact; we do not own or operate these production facilities.

Vision

To build BASUS International into a trusted global commercial supply partner connecting qualified Indian manufacturing capabilities with industrial customers worldwide.

Mission

To simplify international industrial supply by coordinating customer requirements, qualified manufacturing selection, commercial quotations, quality documentation, production follow-up and cross-border delivery for custom electrical components.

Quality & Compliance

Defined by the requirement.
Verified for the project.

Manufacturing partners are evaluated against project-specific quality, technical and documentation requirements. Relevant certifications, test records and compliance documents are verified according to the selected manufacturer and customer application.

Quality Management

ISO 9001

Where required, review of the manufacturing partner’s quality management certificate, validity and scope for relevant manufacturing and inspection processes.

Automotive Quality

IATF 16949

Where required by an automotive or commercial EV project, review of the partner facility’s applicable automotive quality management certification and scope.

Environmental

ISO 14001

Review of the partner facility’s environmental management certification where specified by the customer, including its validity and scope.

Electrical Standards

IEC / Product-Specific Standards

Applicable IEC, UL or DIN requirements are identified for the product and application. Required evidence for electrical ratings, conductivity and dielectric insulation is agreed per project.

Customer Compliance

Customer-Specific Standards

Coordination of material test certificates, dimensional inspection reports and customer-specific requirements. Production Part Approval Process (PPAP) documentation is subject to project and partner scope.

Compliance Disclaimer: These standards describe project-specific qualification criteria, not certifications held by BASUS International. Any certification or test evidence must apply to the selected manufacturing partner, facility and product scope. Availability and verification of documentation are confirmed for the project; no blanket product compliance is implied.

Supply Process

From your drawing
to agreed delivery.

We provide a disciplined commercial supply framework—from initial engineering drawing review through manufacturing partner qualification, quality coordination and agreed international delivery.

01

Share Your Requirement

Technical drawing, specifications, quantity, standards and delivery requirements are reviewed with your engineering and procurement team.

02

Manufacturing Qualification

Suitable Indian manufacturing capability is evaluated against the specific technical, tooling, and volume requirements of the project.

03

Commercial Proposal

Pricing, minimum order quantity (MOQ), tooling, lead time, documentation and delivery terms, including the agreed Incoterms rule, are set out in the commercial proposal.

04

Production & Quality Coordination

Production progress, inspection requirements and required documentation are coordinated with the selected manufacturer throughout manufacturing.

05

International Supply

BASUS manages the commercial supply process, documentation verification, and cross-border logistics through to the agreed delivery arrangement.

Industries We Serve

Components for Electrification & Power

We work with requirements from original equipment manufacturers, Tier-1 suppliers and system integrators. Component suitability is reviewed for each application.

01

EV Charging

High-current DC busbars, power cable assemblies and flexible interconnects for EV charging infrastructure and power distribution units. Liquid- or air-cooled cable requirements need project-specific feasibility review.
